OBSERVATION 001 / LIGHT POLLUTION

Under old street lamps, the pavement takes on the texture of a photograph left in developer. Every wet surface holds a second, trembling street.

Best witnessed
After rain, before 02:00
Field instrument
A window, left uncurtained
OBSERVATION 002 / REPETITION

A lit rectangle on an otherwise dark block. Inside, the slow orbit of a bedsheet. Someone is beginning again, one rinse cycle at a time.

Best witnessed
From across the street
Field instrument
Patience, preferably seated

OBSERVATION 003 / DEPARTURE

The board keeps its promises to nobody. Times advance with the solemnity of a clock in an empty room. Somewhere, a bus is turning around.

Best witnessed
At the terminus
Field instrument
A ticket you don't need
OBSERVATION 004 / OCCUPATION

A single square of light, high above the pavement. A person stands at a counter. The kettle clicks off. The whole block feels briefly inhabited.

Best witnessed
From a respectful distance
Field instrument
An excellent imagination
OBSERVATION 005 / THRESHOLD

Before dawn has a colour, it has a sound: a metal shutter lifted, a crate set down, wheels crossing a seam in the road. The day arrives by hand.

Best witnessed
At the loading bay
Field instrument
A warm coat

06 / THE METHODOPEN
ENDED

How to be
out there.

No equipment list. No route to follow. Just a few principles for noticing the city when its usual performance is over.

  1. 01

    Walk slowly enough for the details to catch up.

  2. 02

    Look at the edges: loading bays, side streets, lit stairwells.

  3. 03

    Do not turn another person's solitude into your spectacle.

  4. 04

    Leave the place exactly as you found it. Leave no trace but attention.
