EMBERLINE

Wren, lantern courier of the Ember Alley, must clear three waves of rogue market-bots before the Overseer seals the Gate. Strike fast, heavy-hit through shields, dash through danger, and block what you cannot dodge.

How to play: reach the bots, beat them. Rust Skitters lunge after a flash. Bulwark Brutes block light hits — heavy hits (K) or striking after their slam breaks them. The Overseer mixes both — watch for the ground mark and the charge.
