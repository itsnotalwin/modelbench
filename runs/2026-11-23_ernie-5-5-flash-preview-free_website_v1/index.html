01 / Statement

Penumbra is the partial shadow — the region where light is only partly blocked. It is the most common condition of the visible world and the least discussed.

A photograph is mostly grey. So is a shadow, a stone, a winter field, the inside of an eyelid. Colour is the exception. The interval is the rule.

This exhibition is built from that interval. Six studies, each generated from a number, each different every time it is drawn, each restricted to the eleven values between chalk and ink. There is no image file anywhere on this page. What you are looking at is arithmetic, resolved in your browser at the moment you arrived.

The constraint is the subject. Remove hue and the remaining decisions — weight, interval, edge, density, the exact position of a line — stop being decoration and become the entire argument. A grey scale is not a limitation on form. It is form, with nothing left to hide behind.

“Colour is what you notice first.
Grey is what you remember.”

04 / Colophon

How it is made

Four notes on the construction of this page. There is no build step, no framework and no image file.

  1. 01

    Method

    Every image on this page is drawn at the moment you look at it. A seeded generator picks the numbers, value noise decides where the ink goes, and a threshold turns it into a mark. Nothing is fetched. Nothing is pre-rendered. Nothing is a photograph.

  2. 02

    Palette

    Eleven greys, from chalk to ink. No hue appears anywhere on this page — not in the type, not in the rules, not in the shadows, not in the grain, not in the favicon. The restriction is absolute and it is the entire design.

  3. 03

    Type

    A serif carries the voice, a grotesque carries the facts, a monospace carries the coordinates. All three are whatever your system already owns, which is why the page weighs almost nothing and renders the same everywhere.

  4. 04

    Motion

    Movement is used to explain, never to decorate. Ask your system for less motion and this page gives you less motion. Ask for none and it stops entirely — every drawing still renders, nothing is hidden behind an animation.
