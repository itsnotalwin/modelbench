Field Guide

An atlas keeps itself; you are only its weather. Grass grows where rain has been. Grazers eat, flee, and breed — their stride, size, sense and vigour are inherited, with small errors. Hunters eat the grazers. Fire eats everything dry. Nothing else is written.
