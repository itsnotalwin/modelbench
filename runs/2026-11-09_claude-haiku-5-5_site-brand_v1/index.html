Four steps, one blade at a time.

Every knife follows the same routine, from a $9 paring knife to a hand-forged santoku. Nothing is ground on a belt, and nothing goes back without a test cut.

  1. 1

    Book

    Tell us how many knives are coming and the earliest day the courier can collect. We confirm the slot within one working day.

  2. 2

    Assess

    We look at each edge under magnification, note chips and rust, and tell you before we start about anything we can’t fix.

  3. 3

    Hone

    We set the bevel by hand on stones from 1000 to 6000 grit, then strop the edge to remove the wire burr.

  4. 4

    Return

    Each knife is tested on printer paper and a ripe tomato, photographed, and boxed for the courier back to you.

The questions people ask before they post a knife.

Will you change the shape of my knife?

Only if you ask. An edge refresh keeps the existing profile. If the blade is thinned or chipped, we give you the cost of restoring the shape before we start.

Do you sharpen serrated or ceramic knives?

We don’t sharpen serrations. The scallops need a tapered rod and a steady hand that a stone can’t give. Ceramic can’t be honed on stones, so we tell you at assessment whether it is worth sending elsewhere.

What happens if a knife can’t be saved?

We stop, send you photos of the damage, and return the knife as it arrived. You pay nothing for the assessment or the return.

How are the knives packed?

Each blade is wrapped in a paper sleeve with the edge covered, then boxed with cardboard guards. Knives longer than 250 mm travel in their own box.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes, free of charge, up to 24 hours before the courier slot. Quote the reference from your booking summary when you get in touch.
