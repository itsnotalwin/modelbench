Portfolio / Plates 01–33

Selected
stories.

Real people, real moments. Work with a focus on character, light and environment — four series, read as a contact sheet or plate by plate.

View

Contact sheet: browse the wall, click a plate for the viewer — ← → Esc. Reading room: the plates sequence full-bleed, paired and single, with full captions.

I

The Green Room

10 plates

Studio studies in a black top and a pale skirt against hand-painted green. The colour drops away in print; what stays is posture, line and the space a figure holds.

II

Sitting Light

8 plates

Chairs, window light and warm interiors. Portraits made slowly, indoors, where a sitter settles and the face does the talking.

III

Open Air

11 plates

Location portraits across Gauteng: overcast water, low sun, wind. Black and white where the light does the talking, colour left at the studio.

IV

Sea Level

4 plates

The places between sessions. Horizons, harbours and dusk skylines kept in the same monochrome register as the portraits.

About these plates.

Every frame here is Alwin Newman’s, made on session work across Gauteng. The four series, the plate numbers and the titles are the studio’s editorial arrangement of that work for this presentation. Photographs are shown in monochrome, as they hang in the studio’s own reference set.
