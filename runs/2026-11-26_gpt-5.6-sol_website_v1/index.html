04 / A note on waiting

The long second.

There are seconds you spend and seconds you endure.

A loading bar, a ringing phone, a door that has not opened yet: the clock gives each one the same unit. Experience does not.

This little instrument cannot tell you why time stretches. It can only make the stretch visible.

Try this

Run one attempt while relaxed, then another after reading something urgent. Do not chase a better score. Compare the shape of the two moments instead.

You do not have time. You notice it.
