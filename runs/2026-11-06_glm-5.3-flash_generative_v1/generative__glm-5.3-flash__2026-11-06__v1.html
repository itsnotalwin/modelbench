Each state is addressed by its seed in the URL — share the link and someone opens the exact same sky. Every state places its own moon and horizon, and the paper is re-graded in every movement, forever varied on each return. Left alone, the piece drifts to sleep; any touch wakes it. Saved prints carry a title, the seed and a small sigil.
