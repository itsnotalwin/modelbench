01 / Our fire

We don't have a signature dish. We have a signature way of paying attention.

Cinder & Salt began as a bet between two people who were tired of food that shouted. In 2016 we took a derelict harbour workshop, built a hearth from reclaimed brick, and decided that the fire would decide the menu. What arrives on the boat, what comes out of the garden, what the jars have been quietly turning into — that is the card we write each morning.

The room is small. The lights are low. The bread is torn, never sliced. Nothing is wasted, and nothing leaves the kitchen before it is ready.

“I keep a fire in the corner of the kitchen. Everything that can touch it, eventually does. That's the whole idea.”

Ingrid Voss · Chef & co-founder
