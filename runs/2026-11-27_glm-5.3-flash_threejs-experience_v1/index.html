Calling the deep

LUMEN DEEP

A bioluminescent reef where light is the only weather. Release a lure — the deep will answer.

Drag to look · scroll / pinch to glide · click / tap the water to release a lure · tap a jellyfish to make it bloom

How to explore

Mouse

  • Drag — look around the reef
  • Right-drag — slide the view
  • Wheel — glide closer or farther
  • Click water — release a lure of light; the school gathers and swirls around it
  • Click a jellyfish — make it bloom

Touch

  • One finger — look around
  • Two fingers — pinch to glide, drag to slide the view
  • Tap — lure or bloom, as above

Keyboard

  • ← → ↑ ↓ — look
  • + / − — glide closer / farther
  • L — release a lure · D — drift · M or Space — motion · H — this panel

If your system prefers reduced motion, the scene starts gentle: drift off and slow currents. You can always switch with Motion.

The reef needs WebGL

Your browser or device has blocked or lacks WebGL, so the living reef cannot be rendered here.

What you would see: a circle of dark dunes 40 paces wide, lit from above by a cold, weak sun. Branching coral in muted rose and teal, beds of small lights that breathe in and out, kelp swaying to a current you cannot feel, and marine snow drifting down like slow static. A school of a hundred-odd silver fish wheels through the middle distance, and six jellyfish hang in the water, pulsing. Release a lure of light and the school abandons its circuit to swirl around it.
