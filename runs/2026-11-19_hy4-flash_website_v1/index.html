Manifesto

A position, in three parts

Most design is an argument for more. More colour, more noise, more proof that someone was here. We are interested in the opposite: the moment a page becomes quiet enough to say one thing clearly.

I.

Subtract until the idea is visible.

Every mark has a cost. Remove what merely decorates and what remains has to carry its own weight. Restraint is not a style here; it is the editing process.

II.

A grid is a promise, not a cage.

Measure twice, then break the measure once. The system gives the eye somewhere to rest; the exception gives it somewhere to go.

III.

Empty space is a material.

We treat the paper as an active substance, not a background. What is left blank is as deliberately chosen as what is printed.

Grammar

Three working materials
01 — GRID

Measure before feeling.

A column system is the quiet argument under every composition. It lets a single interruption read as intention rather than accident.

02 — INK

One colour, fully committed.

Black on white does not need help. Density, scale and rhythm do the work that a palette would otherwise conceal.

100% K / 0% CMY · NO GRADIENT

03 — SILENCE

Leave the page somewhere to breathe.

Whitespace is the pause between two notes. Without it, even a good idea becomes an unreadable crowd.
