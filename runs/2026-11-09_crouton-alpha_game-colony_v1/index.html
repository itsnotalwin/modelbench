How to play Ember Hollow

Goal: Grow to 20 colonists, then build the Sky Beacon to win (then keep playing freely).

Build: Pick a card (1–9), click flat land. Construction takes time and uses idle workers.

Placement matters: Farms near water, Lumber near trees, Quarries near rocks, Taps near crystals produce faster (efficiency shown on ghost + in panel).

Systems: Food feeds everyone. Housing caps population. Depots raise storage. Workshops boost production +25% each (max 2). Upgrades raise capacity/output.

Camera: Drag orbit, wheel zoom, right-drag pan, WASD pan. Space pauses, 1/2/4 change speed, Esc cancels placement.
