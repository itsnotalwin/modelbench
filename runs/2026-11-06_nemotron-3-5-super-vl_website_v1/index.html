About

MONO. is a practice in restraint.

Every pixel on this site is black, white or a shade of grey — in the type, the UI, the imagery, the shadows and the favicon. There is no accent colour, ever.

Constraints are the point. Without colour to lean on, composition, hierarchy, motion and typography have to do all the work. This site is the demonstration: generative drawings made with code, type set with system fonts, layouts built with grid and flexbox, and zero external dependencies.

It is static, responsive, keyboard navigable, and honours your operating system's reduced-motion setting.
