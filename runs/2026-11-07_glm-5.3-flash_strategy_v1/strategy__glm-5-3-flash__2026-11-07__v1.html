How to Play

The Chart

You duel the Umbrae on a hexagonal star chart. Every land hex carries an attunement — gold for the Solari, silver for the Umbrae, pale for unclaimed ground.

Aether & Turns

Each turn grants 3 Aether (unspent Aether fades). Most units may take two actions per turn, but only one attack. Move, attack and attune each cost 1.

Concourse — the heart of the duel

An attack deals +1 harm for each hex beside the target attuned in your colour (up to +3). Claim the ground around a foe before striking, or re-attune your own ground to blunt their Concourse. Standing on your own attuned ground also mends 1 at the turn's start.

Wells & Victory

Hold a Well — a unit of yours beside it while none of theirs is — to gain +1 Convergence at each of your turn's starts. First to 7 wins. You may also win by routing every Umbrae, or by breaching their Hearth with any unit. After 30 rounds the higher Convergence takes the chart.

The Four

Warded — all harm taken is lessened by one; strikes back for 1.
Comet4♥ · moves 4 · harm 1
Flourish — a kill returns one Aether. Chain it.
Lens3♥ · moves 1 · harm 2 at range 2–3
Burst — foes beside the target take one less harm. Never retaliates.
Beacon5♥ · moves 2 · harm 1
Reach — attunes two hexes away. Cord — mends adjacent allies each dawn.

Some hexes bear stone ridges (cover: harm taken −1). Torn hexes are void — nothing crosses. Units cannot pass through one another.

The Fallen

Every unit that falls is inked into the sky as a named star for the rest of the duel — and at the end they are joined into a constellation. Press C to overlay the charted trails of all movement this match.
