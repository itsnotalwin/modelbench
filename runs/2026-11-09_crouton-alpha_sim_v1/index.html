State variables — five coupled stocks (all live, all feed the next)

Interpretation: A grows on N and is eaten by Z → Z grows on A and is eaten by F → F grows on Z and loses to harvest & low O₂ → dead biomass recycles to N → photosynthesis from A plus aeration set O₂. No stock evolves alone.

What to watch

Bloom = A climbs while N falls, then A plateaus at carrying capacity. Eutrophication = N stays high, A stays high, O₂ sags below 6. Overfishing = F falls, Z spikes, A collapses. Hypoxia = O₂ under 3.5, fish extra mortality switches on.

Equations & assumptions — the model, exposed

DT = 0.05 day · RK4 (classic 4-stage) · clamp each step to valid ranges
fT = 2^((T−20)/12) · O_sat(T) = 14.652 −0.41022T +0.007991T² −0.000077774T³
growth_A = 0.65·fT·(N/(N+60))·A·(1−A/100)
graz_A→Z = 0.14·(A/(A+20))·Z
pred_Z→F = 0.10·(Z/(Z+80))·F
dA/dt = growth_A − 0.06A − graz
dZ/dt = 1.20·graz·(1−Z/350) − 0.045Z − pred − 0.12·max(0,3−O)·Z
dF/dt = 0.90·pred·(1−F/130) − 0.025F − harvest·F − 0.18·max(0,3.5−O)·F
dN/dt = inflow − 0.60·growth_A + 0.50·(0.06A+0.5·0.045Z+0.8·0.025F) − 0.05N
dO/dt = 2.6·(A/(A+20)) − 0.014A − 0.0035Z − 0.009F − 0.30·0.06A + (0.22+0.9·aer)·(O_sat−O)
  • Units & ranges: A 0–130 mg/L · Z 0–400 /L · F 0–180 kg/ha · O 0–15 mg/L · N 0–500 µg/L. Valid plotting ranges are tighter for legibility.
  • Assumptions: one well-mixed lake, no seasons, Holling-type-II feeding, logistic algae & fish limits, linear nutrient loss, no migration. Real lakes have depth & gradients — this is a teaching model, not a forecast.
  • Stability: RK4 at dt=0.05 is conservative for these rates (<1/day). Clamps prevent negatives/overflow; clamping is counted in Diagnostics, never hidden. NaN states halt with an error instead of silently continuing.
  • Determinism: no randomness in dynamics, no wall-clock dependence inside the solver. Same scenario + inputs + steps = identical trajectory. Visual wiggle is seeded by index+time.

Using the lab

  1. Start Balanced, press Play. Watch A→Z→F oscillate. Note O₂ tracking saturation.
  2. Switch to Eutrophic. Compare nutrient level, bloom height and O₂ sag over 60 days.
  3. Go Custom: raise inflow to 20 while keeping aeration 0. Try to rescue it with bubblers.
  4. Try harvest 0.04. Fish decline, zooplankton flare, algae crash — a trophic cascade.
  5. Freeze with Pause, Step forward, snapshot the interesting day, export CSV and compare runs.
