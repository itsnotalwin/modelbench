How to Keep a Light

The days. Each day gives you six hours. Walk the island — the jetty, the tower, the house, the cliff — talk, search, mend. The first hour at any place is spent; returning there the same day is free.

The nights. From dusk, the light is yours. Pump oil before the reservoir runs low, trim the wick before it chars, true the lens when it wanders. Watch the water: ships, weather, and other things ask questions of a keeper. Keys 1–5 work the apparatus.

The people. Bo Halloran brings stores and opinions. Sel keeps secrets. Tam Reeve wires across the strait. What you say to them changes what they do when the water rises.

The logbook. Everything a keeper learns is written down. Read it. The water gives back more than driftwood, if you notice.
