CONTROLS — Click the 3D window to jack in. WASD move · SHIFT sprint · SPACE jump · mouse look · F enter/exit your Kobori GT (parked at the plaza) · E use market kiosks · ESC release pointer.

ECONOMY — Six commodities trade on live supply/demand. Buy low at one kiosk, ride out to the plaza, check NEUROWEB for demand pulses, sell high. Your trades move the market.

CIVILIANS — NPCs pathfind with A* over the road network, gossip when you get close, and scatter if you drive at them. Hit one and the drones notice: wanted stars mean hunter drones. Bribe your record clean from the terminal (bribe, ₡500).

WEATHER — Acid rain cuts tire grip and thickens the fog. Storms bring lightning. The rain densifies on you in real time.
