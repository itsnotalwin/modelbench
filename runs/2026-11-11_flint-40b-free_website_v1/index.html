Field guide Nº00 — The hour between

It is Dim.

Day ends. Night begins. Between them, the world exhales — and for a few minutes it turns grey. This is the keeper's log, and the instrument that never sleeps.

01 — Arrival

Dusk doesn't arrive. It unzips.

The sun doesn't vanish. It recedes, politely, over hours, like it has somewhere better to be.

Light doesn't switch off — it lowers its voice. This is the hour when the sky stops performing and starts telling the truth, and the keeper stops writing it down.

Light doesn't leave. It just stops announcing itself.

05 — The return

The light comes back.

And it did. Dawn is just dusk that got over it. The keeper made tea. The station hummed. The grey held.
