Work

Series 01 / 07

Salt Wind

People · c. 2025 · 5 plates

Five portraits made against a seafront: hard daylight, a dark jacket, hair lifted by the wind. The city stays soft behind her; the light does the drawing.

Series 02 / 07

Rail

People · c. 2025 · 4 plates

Four frames around a railing, with the sea beyond it. Close, evenly lit and unhurried; the black T-shirt and the long hair carry the tones.

Series 03 / 07

Level Two

Stories · c. 2026 · 4 plates

A concrete car park, strip lights and a green pillar that monochrome turns to slate. Four frames that treat a place nobody photographs as a set.

Series 04 / 07

Plain Faces

People · c. 2025 · 5 plates

Five faces and almost nothing around them. Soft light, no props, the gaze left to settle where it wants.

Series 05 / 07

Cream Jacket

People · 2024 · 2 plates

Two frames from a morning on 18 July 2024: a cream jacket, a quiet road, clear daylight and a ponytail the wind would not leave alone.

Series 06 / 07

Together

People · c. 2026 · 1 plate

A family, close. The Signature session is built for pictures like this one: more time, more than one location, and everyone in the frame.

Series 07 / 07

Coast

Places · c. 2026 · 3 plates

Three frames of the coast with no one in them: a low sun, a far shore, a ship the size of a grain of rice.
