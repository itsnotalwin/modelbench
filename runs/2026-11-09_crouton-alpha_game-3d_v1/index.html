The Nine Star Shards

When the Observatory broke, its star shards scattered across the floating isles. You are the last Skywarden, and the light in your lantern is the only thing keeping the dark moths at bay. Sail the archipelago on your glider — climb the golden thermals, dive for speed, and thread the storm-dark — recover all nine shards, and reignite the Beacon Spire before the moths go out.

Skywarden's Primer

How to fly

Energy exchange is everything. Your glider has no engine. Diving trades height for speed; climbing trades speed for height. Below stall speed (~9 m/s) you sink and the nose drops. Enter a golden thermal — the shimmering column over an isle — to be lifted while keeping your speed. Boost spends energy; gliding and thermals refill it.

Follow the gold diamonds on the compass to the nearest shard. When all nine are aboard, the teal beam marks the Beacon Spire — fly into its light to reignite it. Talon birds patrol the isles and storm-cells shove sideways; a knock costs one light-gem. Touch the cloud-sea and you will be pushed back into the sky.
