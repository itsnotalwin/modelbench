A TWILIGHT EXPLORATION

Vesper Threshold

You are the lantern-warden of a drowned archipelago at dusk. The five Echo Shards that hold back the long night have scattered to ruin, marsh, garden and sky. Cross the tidal flats, wake each shard, and rekindle the Dawn Gate in the heart plaza.

Explore freely. Pull the drawbridge lever, send an Echo Pulse to reveal light-bridges, sound the rune to open the garden, and raise the Sky Steps with wind-chimes. Gather 12 Memory Motes to piece together why the Threshold was sealed.

Progress auto-saves in your browser. No account needed.
