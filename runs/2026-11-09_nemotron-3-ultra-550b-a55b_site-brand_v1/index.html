Engineering

Why planar magnetic matters

Traditional dynamic drivers move air from a single point. Planar drivers move the entire diaphragm at once. The result: lower distortion, faster transient response, and a presentation that sounds like the microphone feed, not a speaker.

14.2 nm Diaphragm

Our proprietary nano-laminate diaphragm is 1/700th the thickness of a human hair. It responds to the signal, not its own mass. Distortion below 0.05% at 100 dB SPL.

N55 Double-Sided Array

Two mirror-image N55 neodymium magnet arrays create a perfectly uniform 1.2 T field across the entire diaphragm. No dead zones. No field collapse at excursion limits.

Hand-Matched Pairs

Every driver pair is measured at 12 frequencies and matched within ±0.3 dB. Imaging center stays rock-solid. You hear the mix, not the headphone.

CNC Aluminum Chassis

Each cup is machined from a single billet of 6061-T6 in 14 operations. Internal damping chambers are integral, not glued. Resonance pushed above audible range.

Craft

Fourteen days from billet to box

We don't assemble. We build. Every AURA headphone passes through the same hands, in the same room, on the same bench. Here is what those fourteen days look like.

1

Material Selection

6061-T6 aluminum billets, N55 magnet blocks, and diaphragm film arrive with mill certificates. We verify every lot. Rejected material goes back — no exceptions.

2

CNC Machining

14 operations per cup. Internal damping chambers, magnet recesses, and cable tunnels cut in a single setup. Tolerances held to ±0.015 mm. Chips recycled in-house.

3

Driver Assembly

Diaphragm tensioned to 0.8 N/mm² using a custom fixture. Magnet arrays shimmed to 5 μm parallelism. Voice coil trace bonded with medical-grade adhesive. Cured 24 hours at 60 °C.

4

Pair Matching

Each driver measured at 20 Hz–20 kHz in 1/12-octave steps. Pairs matched within ±0.3 dB across the band. Mismatched drivers are repurposed for measurement fixtures.

5

Final Build

Cups mated to headband. Cables terminated with 4.4 mm balanced plugs (Aeon/Verse) or USB-C (Echo). Every screw torqued to spec. Serial number laser-etched.

6

Burn-In & QA

48 hours of pink noise at 90 dB SPL. Final frequency response, THD, and impedance sweep documented. Unit ships with its own measurement sheet, signed by the builder.

Our Story

Built by people who couldn't find what they needed

In 2018, our founder Maya Torres was mastering a record at a major studio. The session's reference headphones — a $4,000 flagship — had a 4 dB peak at 3 kHz that made every vocal sound harsh. The manufacturer called it "voicing." Maya called it a lie.

She spent two years learning planar magnetic design, machining, and driver fabrication. The first Aeon prototype was built on a borrowed CNC in a garage in Northeast Portland. It measured flat to 0.5 dB. The second prototype sounded like music.

Today, AURA is a team of twelve: four machinists, three driver technicians, two assemblers, one measurement engineer, one operations lead, and Maya. We build everything in our 4,200 sq ft workshop on SE 12th. We don't outsource. We don't drop-ship. We don't take venture capital.

Honest measurement

Every unit ships with its own frequency response, THD, and impedance sweep. No "typical" curves. Your serial number, your data.

Time over volume

We build 300 pairs a year. Each takes fourteen days. We will not scale past what our bench can verify.

Repairable by design

Every component is user-replaceable with basic tools. Drivers, pads, cables, headband — all available indefinitely. No planned obsolescence.
