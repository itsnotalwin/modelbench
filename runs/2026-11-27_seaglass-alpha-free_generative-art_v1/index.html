Nocturne·Field

generative artwork · one file · zero assets

A night field of luminous particles advected by a deterministic curl-noise flow. It evolves through four movements — Still Air, Interlace, Tide, Retreat — and never repeats. Everything you see is generated in code; the piece is fully reproducible from a seed held in the URL hash.

Try P to save a high-resolution PNG. Steer the field with pointer or arrow keys. Let it sit still and watch closely.
