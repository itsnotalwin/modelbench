Our Story

Brewed with Love, Served with Vibes

Tucked into the heart of Kharagpur, KGP Vibes Cafe began with a simple idea: a warm, welcoming space where students, families and travellers could slow down over a genuinely great cup of coffee.

From single-origin espresso to charcoal-grilled kebabs and sizzling brownies, everything on our menu is made fresh to order. Come for the coffee, stay for the ambience — and leave as a regular.
