Monochrome

A study in black and white

About

This website is a deliberate exploration of what's possible within a strictly monochrome palette. Every element — typography, spacing, shadow, and form — works within black, white, and the shades of grey between them.

No accent colours. No imagery borrowed from elsewhere. No build step, no framework, no dependencies. Just clean HTML, CSS, and the creative choices that emerge when you strip away the noise.
