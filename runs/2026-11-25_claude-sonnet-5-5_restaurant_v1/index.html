Ashfall is a wood-and-coal kitchen on Fox Street, Johannesburg. No gas, no shortcuts, a lot of smoke.

Book a tableOrder for collection

We cook the way our grandmothers did, on purpose.

Ashfall started as a Sunday braai that got out of hand. Nandi Khumalo, our chef, wanted a kitchen where the fire is the only seasoning you can’t buy: oak and sekelbos in the morning, coal by dinner, nothing sped up.

Meat is dry-aged in-house. Vegetables get the same respect as the ribeye, and often more of the flame. Pap comes with everything it should.

Come hungry. Stay for the second round of embers.

“If a dish doesn’t smell like the fire when it lands, we send it back. We have never once had to send back a dessert.”Nandi Khumalo, chef and owner
