    How to play Embervale

    Goal: Gather 3 Crystal Shards from glowing Crystal Ore, climb the snowy peak, and activate the purple Sky Beacon. Lighting it unlocks permanent flight.

    Mine by looking at a block and holding left click. Blocks drop into your inventory. Place with right click on the face you are looking at. Your hotbar (bottom) holds the blocks you can place — click a slot, press 1–9, or scroll.

    Craft planks from wood, bricks from stone + dirt, glass from sand, and torches from planks. Crafted materials let you build things the wild world does not provide.

    Survive: Hard falls hurt. Health slowly recovers; if you fall out of the world or run out of health you respawn at the starting meadow with your inventory intact.

    Saving: Every edit and milestone is written to this browser automatically. Reset World (top right) wipes the save and regenerates the valley — it asks for confirmation first.
