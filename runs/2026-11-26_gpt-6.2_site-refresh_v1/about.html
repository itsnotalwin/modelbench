Shutterhaus Visuals is my photography practice, based in Kempton Park, Gauteng.

I work on location, making portraits, family photographs and visual content. The setting is part of the photograph: the way light moves, the atmosphere of a place, the small gestures that happen when a person is comfortable.

For a session, we can talk through the location, the time of day and the images you want to take away before we meet.
