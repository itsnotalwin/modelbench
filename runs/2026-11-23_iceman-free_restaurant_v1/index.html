Our Fire

A room built
around the flame.

BRASA began with a simple obsession: what happens when you stop hiding behind technique and let fire do the talking? Chef-owner Marco DeLuca spent a decade cooking in kitchens from Emilia-Romagna to Oaxaca before coming home to Philadelphia with a singular idea — an osteria where the wood oven is the heart, not the equipment.

We burn white oak and fruitwood. Our dough ferments for 72 hours. The vegetables come from farms we can drive to before lunch service. The wine list leans natural, the lights stay low, and the room smells like smoke and good butter. It's not complicated. It's just cared for.

Every seat in the house can see the hearth. That's on purpose. We want you to feel the heat, hear the crackle, and know that someone is paying attention to your dinner from the first spark to the last plate.

Marco DeLuca
Chef & Owner
