A better kind of clear-out

Moving, downsizing or settling an estate can be a lot to carry. The practical work should make it lighter, not harder.

Not everything needs to go to waste.

We don't treat a home like a pile to be shifted. We make a plan for the things inside it. Useful furniture and household goods are separated for possible rehoming. Items you want to keep are set aside. What cannot be reused is directed to an appropriate disposal route wherever one is available.

That means fewer rushed decisions, fewer surprises on the day and a clearer picture of where everything is going. You stay in control of what leaves. We bring the labour, the sorting plan and the follow-through.

The Good Ground standard

Care is in the small decisions.

A clearance is personal. Our ground rules are designed to protect your time, your belongings and your say in the process.

Nothing leaves by surprise.

We agree the rooms and sorting instructions with you first. Documents, photographs, keys and personal effects are set aside for your review, not treated as general contents.

Extras need your yes.

The written quote is the agreement. If access, volume or disposal needs change, we stop and explain the cost before proceeding. No quiet add-ons at the end of a long day.

Rehoming is handled honestly.

We identify items that may be useful elsewhere, but receiving organisations decide what they can accept. We never describe a disposal run as a donation or promise a second home we cannot confirm.

A practical boundary: we do not handle asbestos, chemicals, medical waste, structural demolition, vehicle removal or specialist valuations. If a job includes any of these, we will say so before booking and recommend that you use a qualified specialist.

The experience we're building for

Good work is quiet.

A well-handled clearance does not need a grand story. It should leave people clearer about what happened, what stayed safe and what comes next.

Before you decide

The sensible questions.

Clear answers make for a better start. If your situation is a little unusual, include it in your brief and we'll scope it honestly.

Ask about your space ↗
Which areas do you cover?

We quote across greater Johannesburg. Travel beyond the metro can be arranged when the job makes sense, and any travel cost is shown in the written quote before you book. Share your suburb in the enquiry form and we'll confirm coverage.

Do I need to be there on clearance day?

We recommend that you or one nominated decision-maker is available at the start to confirm the agreed rooms and set-aside items. You do not need to supervise every lift. For estate clearances, we can agree a simple sign-off process with one family contact.

Can you guarantee that items will be donated?

No, and we would rather be straight about that. We can separate clean, usable goods and seek an appropriate receiving route with your approval, but organisations have their own needs and acceptance rules. Anything not accepted is handled according to the agreed plan.

What if we find something important during the job?

We pause and contact the nominated person. Personal documents, photographs, keys, jewellery and other obvious keepsakes are set aside for review. Tell us about known valuables or sensitive items before the day so we can include them in the handling plan.

How quickly can you book us in?

Dates are confirmed after the scope and quote are agreed. Smaller room resets are simpler to schedule; whole-home and estate clearances may need more lead time. We will offer a realistic date rather than promise a slot we cannot protect.

What happens if the job is bigger than expected?

We stop before doing work outside the agreed scope. We explain what has changed, provide the revised cost and continue only after you approve it. If you decide not to proceed, we complete only the work already agreed and discuss a practical next step.

Do you buy furniture or value an estate?

No. We are a clearance service, not an auctioneer, dealer or valuer. We do not deduct assumed resale value from the price, and we cannot provide probate or insurance valuations. If valuation is important, arrange that with an independent qualified professional before clearance.

How do deposits and cancellations work?

Once the scope is agreed, the booking confirmation sets out any deposit, balance date and cancellation terms for that specific job. Those terms are shown before you accept the booking. We do not take payment through this website.
