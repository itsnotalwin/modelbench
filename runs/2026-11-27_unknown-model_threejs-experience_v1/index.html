Lumen Grove

An original abstract grove. Orbit the camera, then send a pulse of light rippling through the foliage.

3D view unavailable

This experience needs WebGL, which is not available in your browser or device.

Try a modern browser with hardware acceleration enabled, or check that no other application is using the graphics device.

How to explore

Camera — mouse

  • Drag to orbit around the grove.
  • Scroll wheel to zoom in and out.
  • Right-drag, or hold Shift while dragging, to pan.
  • Click a glowing leaf to send a pulse from that spot. (A click is not counted after dragging.)

Camera — keyboard

Focus the 3D view (click it or press Tab), then:

  • ← → or A D — rotate left / right.
  • ↑ ↓ or W S — tilt up / down.
  • + / − — zoom in / out.
  • Shift + arrow keys — pan the view.
  • PageUp / PageDown — zoom in / out.
  • Home or R — reset to the default view.
  • Space or P — send a light pulse.

Touch

  • One finger drag to orbit.
  • Pinch with two fingers to zoom.
  • Two-finger drag to pan.
  • Tap a glowing leaf to send a pulse from that spot.

Controls & motion

  • Pulse sends a wave of light through every leaf.
  • Reset view restores the default camera.
  • Pause motion stops ambient animation; you can still explore and send pulses.
  • Motion speed scales ambient animation; set it to 0 for a still grove.
  • If your system requests reduced motion, ambient animation starts off.

Built with pinned Three.js 0.160.0. No build step, no extra libraries.
