How to play

Goal: survey each chapter's districts, craft its chart, survive the rising tide, and choose the final line.

Daylight: 6 per day. Survey / talk / travel cost 1. At 0, wait for night — tide rises, day advances. If tide hits 100% before you finish, the city floods early: you must craft quickly or lose trust.

Systems talk: dialogue choices move trust and tide. Trust unlocks options and ink trades. Surveys give fragments for crafting. Discoveries give journal lore and ink. All of it changes the ending.

Keys: Tab to move · Enter to choose · S save · H help · Esc pause · 1–7 travel to district · C chart desk · J journal.

Accessibility: reduced-motion and large-text in Settings. Colour is never the only signal — every state is labelled in words.
