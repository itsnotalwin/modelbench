Puzzle board

Welcome, Lightkeeper. Press Start, then slide with the arrow keys, WASD, the D-pad, or a swipe.

How to play

You slide until you hit something. You cannot stop in open space. Plan your stop with walls, crates, honey, mirrors and portals.

Collect every ◆ signal orb, then reach the exit. Sliding over or stopping on the exit after all orbs are lit wins the level.

Controls: ↑↓←→ or WASD to slide · Z Undo · R Restart · H Hint · P/Esc Pause. Touch: D-pad or swipe the board.

Tile legend — what each thing does
  • 🧱 Wall — stops your slide and crate slides.
  • ◉ Honey (teal) — you stop the moment you touch it, even mid-slide. Crates also stop on honey.
  • ╱ ╲ Mirrors — / turns north→east, east→north; \ turns east→south, south→east, etc. You keep sliding after the turn.
  • ◎ Portal — entering one teleports you to its pair and you keep sliding in the same direction.
  • ■ Crate — you push it one slide when you hit it, then you stop where it was. It slides until a wall, sticks on honey, and sinks into pits to fill them.
  • ◯ Pit — sliding into a pit makes you fall and return to the start of that move. Fill pits with crates.
  • ◆ Orb — collected when you slide over it. ▶ Exit is locked until every orb is collected.
