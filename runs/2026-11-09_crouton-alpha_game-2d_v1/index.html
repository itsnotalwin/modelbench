Gravitide

The Reef has gone dark. You are its last courier — sling between gravity wells, carry light home.

Three wells hold the sea together. Void hunters drift in the black and will ram you for the light you carry. Fill a shard run, haul it back to the Beacon, and do it again — faster, deeper, until 24 shards burn again. Dash through a hunter to break it. Lose your hull and the dark keeps you.

SlingSkim a well fast without touching it for a +75 slingshot bonus. Wells pulse — and in the last phase, they surge and repel.
