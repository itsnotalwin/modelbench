Editorial point of view

About the journal →

We read documents as layered objects, not transparent windows. Every text carries an undertext: what was scraped away before it, what was added at its edges, and what each reader leaves behind. Palimpsest publishes essays on recovered writing, annotation, book history, and the quiet crafts of preservation—work that treats erasure as evidence, not absence.

Our voice is scholarly but unhurried; precise but not cold. We prefer small verifiable claims to grand ones, name our sources, and say plainly when something is reconstructed. There is no house style for what a document means; there is a house style for how carefully we say it. Read more about the journal.
