About this issue

Volume I, Number 1, The Undertext issue, published 27 November 2026. The issue pairs a historical feature with a critical essay that answers it from the reader’s side: first what is scraped off a page, then what readers write in the space around it. Both essays carry footnotes, figures, margin notes, and related reading.

There is no archive of prior issues yet; this is Number 1. No paywall, no accounts, no tracking. All artwork is original inline SVG or CSS.
