    Studio

    We remove colour to find the work.

    “A second colour is an excuse. Grey is where honest hierarchy lives.”

    STILL is a small, fictional studio created to demonstrate what careful craft looks like with almost nothing to hide behind. We strip palette, stock imagery and noise away, then rebuild meaning with type scale, rhythm, spacing, contrast and motion.

    The result is calm and unmistakable: pages that read at a glance, print cleanly, work in either light or dark, and never rely on colour to convey meaning. Everything is built as static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript — no frameworks, no CDNs, no tracking, no build step.

    • 01One tone. Black, white and grey only — including shadows, gradients, art and icons.
    • 02Real interaction. Nothing is a placeholder; every button does something and every link goes somewhere.
    • 03Accessible by default. Keyboard, focus, contrast, reduced motion and screen-reader semantics are not optional.
    • 04No dependencies. It opens from a file, serves from any path, and runs offline.

    Services

    What we make.

    • 01

      Identity & type

      Monochrome brand systems: marks, wordmarks, type hierarchy, tone-of-voice, and rules that survive reproduction at any size, in any order.

    • 02

      Editorial & print

      Publications, reports and art books engineered for contrast, readability and rhythm. Designed to look intentional in pure black on white.

    • 03

      Digital systems

      Static-first websites and component libraries: responsive, keyboard accessible, dark/light inversion, and fast without a build pipeline.

    • 04

      Motion & interaction

      Restrained motion that clarifies, honours prefers-reduced-motion, and degrades gracefully. No gratuitous animation.

    • 05

      Generated imagery

      Original art made in code — SVG, canvas and procedural patterns — so nothing is sourced, stocked, or copyrighted elsewhere.

    • 06

      Accessibility audit

      Contrast, focus order, semantics, and reduced-motion review to make monochrome work for every reader.

    Process

    Four moves, then it ships.

    1. 01

      Listen

      We start with the problem, the audience and the one message that must survive. No mood boards before the brief is clear.

    2. 02

      Strip

      We remove every element that isn't earning its place, including — always — colour. What remains is structure.

    3. 03

      Build

      We make it real: responsive, accessible, generated, and shipped as plain files. Reviewable by anyone, forever.

    4. 04

      Refine

      We test keyboard, contrast, reduced motion and reproduction, fix what's weak, and leave a clear record of trade-offs.
