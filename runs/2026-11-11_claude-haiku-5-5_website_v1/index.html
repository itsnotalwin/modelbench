03 · Principles

Three habits of the craft.

  1. Information lives in pattern.

    One bit cannot hold a shade, but a neighbourhood of bits can. Dithering trades resolution for tonal range.

  2. Error is a resource.

    Diffusion keeps what rounding throws away and hands it to the pixels still to come, so the average stays true.

  3. Choose the texture on purpose.

    A regular grid reads as print, diffused error reads closer to a photograph, and a hard cut reads as graphic design.
