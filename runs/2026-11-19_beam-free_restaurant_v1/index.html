Our story

A house of fire and patience.

We cook with wood, cure with time, and pour what the season gives us.

— Mads & Yuki, founders

Komorebi began as a Saturday pop-up in Mads's backyard in Nørrebro — a single grill, twelve seats, and a notebook of ideas Yuki had carried from Osaka. The smoke drifted over the rooftops and the neighbours came down with wine.

Today we live in a former print house on Guldbergsgade. The press is gone; the brick, the light, and the patience remain. Our menu changes every two weeks with what our growers bring in. The fire never goes out.

We are a small room. We would rather be full and honest than large and forgettable.
