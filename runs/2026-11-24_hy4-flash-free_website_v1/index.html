01  /  Position

Restraint is not the absence of something. It is the presence of a choice.

This journal began with a restriction rather than an idea. No colour. No photography. No borrowed images. Every mark on these pages is drawn at runtime by a handful of equations and a deterministic seed. What survives that constraint is worth looking at.

Monochrome is often described as a lack — a missing channel, a world with a piece of itself switched off. We think the opposite is true. Remove hue and the eye starts reading structure: weight, rhythm, edge, interval. The decisions that were camouflaged by colour become visible, and they are either good or they are not.

The six plates that follow are studies in that visibility. Each is generated, not painted. Each can be regenerated, and none is the definitive version. The studio at the end of the journal hands you the same tools.

“When the palette has one colour, every difference is a decision.”
