Our story

Two sisters.
One stubborn kiln.

We grew up in Haifa watching our grandfather rake embers with a bent shovel. When we arrived in London in 2016 we wanted that smell again — smoke, warm pitta, lemon on everything.

So in a rented arch on Mare Street we laid 4,000 bricks, one by one, and built a kiln that reaches 500°C. No gas. No shortcuts. If the dough isn't ready, we wait. If the coals aren't right, the lamb waits — not you, the lamb.

“Bread is our clock. Fire is our language. Everything else is just what we found at the market that morning.”
— Layla & Noor Haddad, founders

Our menu changes with the market and the wood. Fish when the boats come in, greens when the fields can spare them. That's not indecision, that's the point.
